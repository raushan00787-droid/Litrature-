A reflection by Raushan

The Miracle of Being Chosen

“Kafka, you say that to be loved when the whole world feels misaligned is a miracle.

But tell me, what if she already has people who comfort her? People who make her laugh, who stay when the world falls apart?

What if she has never needed my attention to feel whole?

Then tell me, Kafka, what makes my being loved by her a miracle?

If I am not her refuge, nor the reason behind her laughter, nor the hand she reaches for in her darkest hours, what am I?

Perhaps I have mistaken being needed for being loved.

Perhaps the miracle was never that she needed me.

Perhaps it was that, among all the people she could turn to, she could still choose me.
