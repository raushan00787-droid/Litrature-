04 · About Aurelis

A house for
written worlds.

Aurelis is an independent digital literary house for stories, poetry, essays, reflections, fragments, and experiments. It brings different writers into one place without asking them to share one style or one point of view. Every piece has its own voice; the house gives readers a way to find it.

← Back to Aurelis

Different voices.
One house.

Aurelis is a shared home for writing, not a voice that replaces the writers behind it. Works are presented with clear titles and author credits, so the person who wrote each piece remains visible.

01 · The principle

Distinct voices

Different writers can sit side by side without being made to sound alike.

02 · The promise

Clear credit

Each work stays attached to the writer who created it, and readers can browse a writer's catalogue directly.

03 · The purpose

Room to grow

The catalogue can expand with new stories, poems, essays, and experiments while keeping the house organised and welcoming.

Built by Raushan, Aurelis is an ongoing literary project shaped around one simple idea: a shared home can make room for many voices without taking any of them away from their author.
