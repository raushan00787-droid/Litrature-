THE CLOSEST STRANGER01
Chapter One · 🍃

Chapter One

It's two o'clock. The cold breeze keeps striking me through the open window. And here I am, lying in bed, staring at the ceiling, trying to brush away the memories of my past. Tears burn in my eyes and roll down my cheeks as my head grows heavy with thoughts that haunt me every second.

Every night has passed like this ever since that incident took place. I keep trying to forget it, but the scenes from the past refuse to leave me alone.

I remember the days of my childhood, when my father would lose his temper with my mother. Everything had begun after my mother found out about my father's affair with her “best friend”, something she learned because of me.

THE CLOSEST STRANGER02

My father was one of the biggest businessmen in London. He worked on big ideas late at night and sometimes spoke to someone on the phone whom nobody knew about. Yes, I belonged to a family that was rich beyond measure. But nobody saw the darkness of violence behind the glitter of luxury.

I remember the day my father returned home from work and slammed the heavy oak door so loudly that my heart jumped. I was sitting on my bed with my head buried in my knees after seeing my mother break down in tears because of what I had told her.

I had told her about my dad and Aunt Alicia the previous night, when she had gone out to buy him a birthday gift. I didn't understand what was happening between them when I saw them together. I only knew that something was wrong. Neither of them noticed me watching from the edge of the slightly open door.

THE CLOSEST STRANGER03

I was still sitting on my bed when I heard the door slam. Then came my father's voice, raised in anger, followed by my mother's trembling attempts to argue with him.

That was when our house help and my babysitter, Aria, opened the door to my room. I almost jumped out of bed from fright, but I felt an enormous sense of relief when I saw her.

Aria was my best companion. She used to drop me at school and pick me up, feed me with her hands, take care of me, play with me, and comfort me whenever my parents argued in loud voices. Whenever things became frightening at home, she was the person who made me feel safe.

Aria sat beside me on the edge of the bed and pulled me into her arms.

“Hey, darling. Look at me. Listen to me, okay? You are completely safe right now. Right here with me, you are safe.”

THE CLOSEST STRANGER04

I felt comfortable and warm in her arms, and eventually I fell asleep beside her that night.

But the very next day, I woke to the sound of police sirens and an ambulance outside. I got out of bed and went downstairs to see what had happened.

What I saw left me completely stunned.

For a moment, it felt as though I had lost the ability to hear or feel anything. My mother was there with the police, visibly shaken and injured. My father's body lay in the hall near the couch, and the scene around him was enough to make me freeze.

I stood there with my eyes wide open, tears running down my face. I was so shocked and scared that the voices around me seemed distant. I couldn't understand what was happening.

THE CLOSEST STRANGER05

Pieces of an expensive vase were being collected by a police officer as evidence. Then hospital staff entered and carefully covered my father's body before taking him away.

My mother stood there with her head bowed, her expression unreadable.

Aria, who had been speaking with the police, noticed me. Her eyes filled with concern as she hurried towards me. She pulled me into her arms and led me back to my bedroom.

My mother was arrested, and a case was filed against her. But she and her lawyer somehow managed to escape all the allegations of killing Mr. Arthur James Sterling. Even after her release, my mother didn't choose to stay with her traumatised daughter, who needed her mother's love. Instead, she moved to Belgium with a new man and left Aria as my guardian.

I have her number, but we barely talk. She calls me twice a year, once on Christmas and once on my birthday. As I have already said, my family is rich, so neither my mother nor I faced any financial crisis.

I was not able to go to school for several months after the incident because I was afraid of people and of my friends asking me about my mom killing my dad. I hated the way they would pity me, calling me “poor baby” or “poor child”. This was one reason, but the other was the trauma I was going through after seeing my dad's body and my mom being taken away in handcuffs.

At that time, Aria was the person who did her best to keep my mind alive and playful. She took me to parks and insisted that I play. Sometimes, she even went down the slides just to encourage me to play, and yes, her efforts worked. I would run towards the slides as soon as I saw her sliding down them.

Her routine was still the same: waking me up, cooking for me, feeding me, giving me a bath, taking me to the park, protecting me from people who wanted to ask me questions about my dad's murder, and, at the end of the day, making me fall asleep in her lap.

THE CLOSEST STRANGER06
Chapter Two · 🌙

Chapter Two

She was like my second mother. When I slowly recovered from my trauma and became prepared enough to go back to school, Aria dropped me off and picked me up from school just like before. I loved her so much. I mean, I still do, and I am so grateful to her for whatever she did for me. But I don't know where she is now. Otherwise, I would have visited her at least once every month with lots of presents, even though they could never pay back what I owed her.

When I turned twelve, my mom bought me a luxurious flat and left one of my friend's families in charge of me. I have not seen Aria since that day.

After the day of that incident, my life changed completely. It became normal for me when I was a child, but all those things started haunting me again when I turned fifteen. Every night since then, I suddenly get hit by those scenes and thoughts. I have lost my ability to make friends. I don't talk much, and of course, people find me boring. I also don't have a best friend, and yes, I am completely lonely.

And now, here I am, lying in bed, looking at the ceiling and thinking about my past at 2 AM. I don't know when I slipped into sleep.

Oh my godddddd!!! It's 9 a.m. I am expected to be at the school campus by this time, but I have just woken up. I quickly go to the bathroom with a brush in my mouth, brushing my teeth, and a towel in one hand. I come out fifteen minutes later, rubbing moisturiser on my face and picking up my bag full of colour markers and project papers.

I quickly rush to the door, lock it, and head to the lift. As I enter the lift, I come face-to-face with Julia. She is late too, so now I am relieved that I won't be the only one to get insulted in front of the whole class by the teacher.

We both reach the school, running and rushing. We both enter the class, expecting Mrs. Tanya to say something, but she simply tells us to go to our seats.

I sit on my chair and start thinking about the day ahead. I know it's going to be a really long one because I have to do my assignment, which is to be submitted by the end of this week. Mrs. Tanya had given us two weeks for this assignment, but I spent a whole one week sleeping and dreaming about the same old thing.

I have thought of doing this work while sitting in the school library after school because it's so peaceful and less distracting. Moreover, there would not be anyone in the library after school, so yeah, it's a good chance for me to complete half of the work before the submission date. Because completing the whole work before the date means two to three free days, which I could enjoy without any pressure.

School ended, and everyone was walking to their homes except for me. I was heading towards the library. As expected, it was completely empty and silent. I would call it peaceful rather than silent.

I got in, sat on a chair, and started taking out all the assignment stuff when my eyes suddenly caught sight of him. He was sitting in the corner of the library near the big window with his head in a book. I couldn't see his face because he was holding a novel with The Silent Patient by Alex Michaelides written in big letters right in front of his face. But still, even without looking at him, I found him charming.

He was wearing a light blue shirt with brown oversized jeans and earplugs in his ears. I don't understand how people read and listen to music at the same time.

Anyways, then he suddenly brought down the novel from his face and looked at me. I immediately took my eyes off him and looked at my papers and started drawing flowers on them to prepare the front page.

Oh my godddddd!! He is so handsome. I don't remember when I was last attracted to a guy. His jawline, his curly hair, his brown eyes, and his tanned complexion have caught my eyes. I was thinking about his facial features when something unexpected happened.

He came and sat on the chair beside me. My heart was jumping. I looked at him. He was looking at me too. Then he said, “Hey Florence, are you here for the assignment work too?”

I just nodded my head. I was wondering how he knew my name.

Then he said, “How much have you done yet? Actually, I too needed help with it. I think if you can...”

“Yes, of course I will.”

“Oh, thank you so much. I am Jule Martin, by the way.”

“Nice to meet you, Jule. I am Florence Sterling.”

“I know that.”

“How do you?”

“We are in the same grade.”

“Are we? I am so sorry. I never saw you.”

“It's alright. I know you don't notice anyone easily.”

“How do you know that?”

“I have just observed you sometimes, and it gave me the impression that you don't easily mix with people.”

“Yeah.” I gave him a smile. I didn't know what to say.

“So, how should I help you?” I asked.

“Wait, let me get my stuff first.”

He went back to his initial place and came back with his bag. He took out coloured papers from his bag and asked me to help him prepare a beautiful front page. I smiled at him.

We both prepared the project together and, to my surprise, the project we had been given two weeks to complete got completed in just a few hours.

I know it sounds completely unrealistic, but yes, it happened. I don't know how time passed with Jules. When it was time to separate, I felt a bit sad to leave him. But I said bye and started heading back home.

He said, “I will see you tomorrow, Florence.” The way he said “Florence” made my heart melt. I think I am getting attracted towards him. Or maybe I am falling in love with him...

THE CLOSEST STRANGER07
Chapter Three · 💌

Chapter Three

I open the door and get in. I need a shower. I am exhausted after all the work, but I am still happy that I met Jules because of it. I hope I have left a good impression on him. I am excited to meet him tomorrow. I have never been attracted to a guy before. How did Jule manage to get my heart?

Anyways, I am just really delighted to meet him. I take a hot shower and go to the kitchen. I hate cooking after coming back from school, especially when I have overworked myself. But today, all the exhaustion has disappeared.

I go to the kitchen and get myself a bowl of chicken noodle soup with a can of Diet Coke. This is life. Sitting casually with my blanket all over my body, my hot bowl of soup, a can of Diet Coke, and Netflix. I completely forget about my past at such moments.

It's the last episode of the show. As soon as I finish the show, I get up with the dishes and wash them. Then, I stretch my body on the bed, thinking about Jule. The way he talks, gets shy, and covers his face with both his hands when I ask him if he has a girlfriend.

I have never gotten so friendly with anyone except Aria. Now, I think I have found someone who can make me feel like I have a friend.

I don't know when I slipped into a deep sleep.

Without any nightmare.

THE CLOSEST STRANGER08
Chapter Four · 🌨️

Chapter Four

I get up as soon as the alarm rings. I don't remember when I last woke up to the sound of my alarm. I don't feel tired or sleepy after waking up, unlike regular days. I go to the bathroom and look at myself in the mirror. My skin is glowing. I have not seen my skin glow like this in a decade. I quickly take out my phone and take a picture of myself. I am not much of a photo person, but for the first time, I feel that this face of mine deserves to be captured on camera.

I brush my teeth, take a shower, and get dressed in my light purple sweater and light blue jeans with my white sneakers. Thankfully, I don't have any bullies at school. I am excited to go to school for the first time. I lock the door and get into the lift. Now I am heading towards my school. My heart is filled with enthusiasm. I wonder if Jule finds me attractive. I mean, I am just curious. Is there a way? Ahhhh!! I don't know, but I think I am going to give my best at it.

I am standing in the school campus, looking for Jule. He suddenly comes from behind and says, “Hey, Florence!”

I turn around. Doesn't he have any friends? I mean, there is no one with him. Should I ask him?

“Good morning, Jule. Where are your friends?” I finally ask.

“I don't have friends, Flora...” he says.

“Oh, I am sorry.” The way he called me Flora has melted my heart. I am truly in love with him.

“Would you be my friend?”

“Uh... of course I am your friend. Do we still need to ask to be friends?”

“Thanks, Flora.” Again, he called me that. I think tears will flow down his eyes. I want to hug him, kiss him, and make him feel safe.

We both go to the classroom together. I know there must be a lot of girls crushing over him. And seeing him with me can cause some problems for me. But I don't care. I can handle everything for him.

We sit side by side. After the first class ends, he asks me to spend time in the library after school ends. He tells me about the novel he was reading yesterday. I agree with him. We both decide to get chocolate smoothies from “Vellum”, the nearest café to our school, and sit in the library talking and doing assignments.

I can't tell how blessed I am feeling to be friends with Jule. I mean, all I wanted was to be at least in his list. And now I have become the only friend of his. I think Jesus has some good plan for me.

I am walking with Jule to the library. School has ended, and everyone is walking out of the school campus. The golden rays of the sun are hitting Jule's face, and it's making him look like a main character or a prince from a fantasy story.

We both keep our bags on the corner seat where Jule was sitting the previous day. It was quite a comfortable place. It was a seat for four, with two comfortable two-seater couches and a big window. A pink blossom tree was there, right outside the window. I wonder why I didn't come here earlier.

We leave our bags there and, after telling the library in-charge that we are going out for a minute, we head towards Vellum.

Vellum is my favourite café of all time. Warm lights and candles make the whole café comfortable and cosy. The smell of coffee, chocolates, cream, and pastries is always flowing through the air. The warm-hearted staff, their smiles, everything makes my day a little better after I enter this café.

Jule and I enter the café and sit at a table near the window. Jule has some kind of obsession with windows, I guess.

We order rich cocoa chocolate smoothies and croissants. Obviously, we have to get these things packed to take them to the library. But still, instead of standing at the counter, Jule decides to show me the beautiful winter view of London outside the café through the window.

The view is really mesmerizing.

By this time, I have got to know that Jule is a keen observer because I have been to Vellum a hundred times, but I have never noticed these things that he has.

Our stuff gets packed, and we go back to the library. We enter the library and sit in our place. I am taking the chocolate smoothie and croissant out of the carriage bag when I find Jule looking at me with his innocent eyes.

This man is making me fall in love with him again and again.

I look at him and ask him if he needs something, but he just nods his head in “no”. I then hand him the smoothie.

Taking a sip from it, he says, “Flora, I will call you this from now on.”

“I like it.”

I love it when he calls me that, and now he is giving it to me as my nickname. Wow!

“So, tell me, Flora, why don't you have any friends?”

I think it's a strange question. I start telling him the whole journey of my life, about my dad getting killed by my mom and my mom escaping the allegations and getting shifted to another place, leaving me with a house help.

I tell him every single detail of whatever has happened to me. I see him getting emotional. I have never shared these details with anyone, but I don't know why I am trusting him so much even though I have just met him yesterday.

Jule looks at me and says, “Flora, I am really sorry to hear all this. I don't know if I am enough, but I will give my best to make you feel safe.”

These words touched each corner of my heart. For the first time, I have got a friend, and that too such a caring and genuine one. I am really, really blessed. I thank the stars for making me meet the destination of my life.

We discuss about each other's family. I tell him I have no family and I am living alone in my big flat. Jule lives with his family, which includes his mom, his dad, and his 10-year-old brother. He has a happy life and a happy family.

One reason he tells me for not having any friends is past betrayals. His best friend had left him because he had found new friends. Jule is an introvert, and that's why he only had one best friend. After getting betrayed, he never looked back to make any further friends.

He tells me about his future plan of starting a business. He asks about mine. I tell him I want to be a psychiatrist. He smiles at this.

After talking for some hours, we realise it has got too late. The light has already faded, and the library in-charge informs us that the library's closing time is here.

We both get out of the library. After observing the time and the weather, he decides to walk with me to my home. I tell him not to do so and that I am good on my own, but he still walks with me until I reach my building.

I invite him inside, and he accepts it.

We both get inside my flat. I make him sit on the couch and ask him if his parents might be worrying about him at home. He says he is calling them to let them know that he is with a friend and will be home by 11 p.m.

It's already 9 p.m.

I ask him if he would like some chicken soup. He replies in the affirmative, and I get into the kitchen to prepare it for us.

Meanwhile, he keeps moving from one room to another, observing everything. I don't know what he is thinking.

I get the chicken soup ready and serve it on the table. I switch on the TV. I sit beside him, a bit distant.

We both enjoy watching my favourite show, and then I say good night and goodbye to Jule before he leaves for his home.

This day was something we can call magical. I didn't even dream of Jule getting into my flat and us watching Netflix together. I thank God for whatever is happening and slip into my bed for a deep sleep.
